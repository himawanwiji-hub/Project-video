# Prompt chibi: reporter cewek membawakan berita

Prompt untuk generator gambar/video AI (Midjourney, Leonardo, Ideogram, Kling, Runway, Pika, Veo, Sora).
Berita yang dibawakan: materi **Soal 2 (Motor)** dan **Soal 4 (Generator AC)**, dikemas seperti siaran berita
televisi. Stasiunnya fiktif: **"Berita Fisika TV"**. Kerangkanya juga bisa dipakai untuk berita lain (lihat bagian
paling bawah).

**Cara pakai:** gabungkan **[GAYA]** + **[REPORTER]** + satu prompt adegan, rasio 16:9 (atau 9:16 untuk
Reels/TikTok). Teks di layar (judul berita, running text) sering salah ditulis AI, jadi tambahkan saat editing.

---

## [GAYA]

```
chibi cartoon style, cute super-deformed characters with big heads (2-head-tall proportions), large sparkly eyes,
rosy cheeks, soft rounded shapes, thick clean outlines, flat pastel colors with soft cel shading, kawaii anime
TV news broadcast look, bright studio lighting, clean composition, high detail, 16:9
```

## [REPORTER] (pilih salah satu dan pakai terus agar konsisten)

**Versi A: berhijab**
```
Rara the reporter: a cheerful chibi Indonesian female news anchor, pastel pink hijab, big sparkly brown eyes,
navy blue blazer with a small gold lightning-bolt pin, white blouse, holding a cute microphone with a yellow
mic flag, confident friendly smile.
```

**Versi B: rambut panjang**
```
Rara the reporter: a cheerful chibi Indonesian female news anchor, long dark brown hair in a high ponytail with a
yellow ribbon, big sparkly brown eyes, navy blue blazer with a small gold lightning-bolt pin, white blouse,
holding a cute microphone with a yellow mic flag, confident friendly smile.
```

**Tokoh pendukung (opsional)**
```
Pak Dosen: a cheerful chibi lecturer with round glasses and a light blue batik shirt, holding a pointer stick.
Elektron: a tiny round glowing yellow mascot with a happy face, representing electric current.
```

## Negatif

```
realistic, photorealistic, 3d render, creepy, extra fingers, deformed hands, blurry, messy text, real TV channel
logo, watermark, dark gloomy
```

---

## Adegan

**1. Pembukaan di studio**
```
Rara sits at a sleek pastel news desk in a cute TV studio, a big screen behind her shows a cartoon electric motor
and a lightning bolt, blue and yellow studio lights, a cute "LIVE" badge in the corner; she smiles at the camera
with hands folded on the desk.
Dialog: "Selamat malam, pemirsa. Kembali di Berita Fisika. Malam ini kami mengungkap rahasia di balik motor
dan generator."
```

**2. Berita utama: kumparan berputar sendiri**
```
Rara stands next to a giant studio screen showing a copper coil between a red magnet labeled U and a blue magnet
labeled S, with a green arrow pushing up on one side and a green arrow pushing down on the other; she points at
the screen with a tablet in her other hand, Elektron mascots run around the coil on the screen.
Dialog: "Berita utama: sebuah kumparan berputar tanpa disentuh. Penyebabnya, dua sisinya didorong berlawanan
arah oleh gaya magnet."
```

**3. Laporan langsung: dinamo kepanasan (breaking news)**
```
Rara reports on location in a cute workshop, holding her microphone, behind her a small motor glows red-hot with
cartoon steam puffs because a hand is holding its shaft; a red flashing "BREAKING NEWS" banner, a thermometer
character sweating; Rara looks surprised with one hand on her cheek.
Dialog: "Pemirsa, poros dinamo ini ditahan. Arusnya melonjak dari setengah ampere menjadi tiga ampere, dan
panasnya naik tiga puluh enam kali!"
```

**4. Wawancara pakar**
```
Rara interviews Pak Dosen in a cozy lab, she holds the microphone toward him, he explains while pointing at a
small chalkboard with a sine wave doodle; two chibi students peek from behind the door; warm afternoon light.
Dialog (Rara): "Pak, kenapa gayanya bisa nol?"
Dialog (Pak Dosen): "Kalau kawat sejajar medan, gayanya nol. Jangan lupa sin teta!"
```

**5. Laporan dari pembangkit listrik**
```
Rara stands in front of a cute cartoon power plant with a waterfall dam on one side and a wind turbine on the
other, wearing a yellow safety helmet over her outfit, holding the microphone; a giant spinning generator coil
glows behind a glass window; small icons of coal, water, geothermal steam, wind and a diesel can float around.
Dialog: "Saya berada di pembangkit listrik. Apa pun bahan bakarnya, tugasnya sama: memutar kumparan di dalam
medan magnet."
```

**6. Segmen "prakiraan" frekuensi (gaya ramalan cuaca)**
```
Rara stands beside a big weather-map style screen, but instead of clouds it shows cute sine waves over a map of
Indonesian islands, with a big friendly badge "50 Hz" and small rotor icons with 4 and 24 poles; she gestures
like a weather presenter with a cheerful wink.
Dialog: "Prakiraan frekuensi hari ini: stabil lima puluh hertz di seluruh wilayah. Rotor empat kutub dan dua
puluh empat kutub sama-sama aman."
```

**7. Segmen konsumen: genset kewalahan**
```
Rara stands next to a chubby generator-set character with a worried face carrying a stack of appliance
characters (lamp, fridge, water pump, air conditioner) on its head, a glowing red dashed "safe limit" line; Rara
switches off the water pump, which hops away happily, and the generator smiles with relief; Rara gives a thumbs up.
Dialog: "Tips untuk pemirsa: kVA bukan kW. Kalau beban lewat delapan puluh persen, matikan salah satu alat."
```

**8. Penutup**
```
Back at the news desk, Rara waves goodbye to the camera, a cute motor character and a cute generator character
hold hands on the big screen behind her with a heart between them, confetti and tiny lightning bolts, studio
lights dimming warmly.
Dialog: "Motor dan generator ternyata mesin yang sama. Saya Rara, dari Berita Fisika. Sampai jumpa!"
```

---

## Prompt gerak (untuk tool video)

Tambahkan di akhir prompt adegan:

```
reporter talks to camera with natural lip-sync and small hand gestures, blinks, slight head tilt,
gentle camera push-in, 5-8 seconds, 24 fps
```

Untuk tool yang bisa membuat suara (misalnya Veo), tambahkan:

```
female Indonesian news anchor voice, clear, warm and energetic, speaking Bahasa Indonesia, soft news music in the
background
```

## Kerangka untuk berita lain

```
[GAYA] + [REPORTER] +
Rara [lokasi: di studio / di lapangan / di depan gedung ...], [aksi: memegang mikrofon, menunjuk layar ...],
behind her [visual berita dalam gaya chibi], [emosi: tersenyum / terkejut / serius].
Dialog: "[kalimat berita, 1-2 kalimat]"
```
