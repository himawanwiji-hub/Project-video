# Prompt gaya kartun chibi: Soal 2 (Motor) dan Soal 4 (Generator AC)

Prompt untuk generator gambar/video AI (Midjourney, Leonardo, Ideogram, DALL·E, Kling, Runway, Pika, Veo, Sora).
Promptnya ditulis dalam bahasa Inggris karena kebanyakan model lebih patuh dengan bahasa Inggris. Dialog tetap
berbahasa Indonesia.

**Cara pakai:** gabungkan **[GAYA]** + **[KARAKTER]** + satu prompt adegan. Pakai rasio 16:9. Teks dan rumus di
papan tulis sering salah ditulis AI, jadi tambahkan teks/rumus saat editing.

---

## [GAYA] (tempel di setiap prompt)

```
chibi cartoon style, cute super-deformed characters with big heads (2-head-tall proportions), large sparkly eyes,
rosy cheeks, soft rounded shapes, thick clean outlines, flat pastel colors with soft cel shading, kawaii anime
educational cartoon, bright cheerful classroom lighting, clean simple background, high detail, 16:9
```

## [KARAKTER] (agar tokohnya konsisten di semua adegan)

```
Pak Dosen: a cheerful chibi Indonesian university lecturer, short neat black hair, round glasses, light blue
batik shirt, dark trousers, holding a pointer stick, friendly smile.
Mahasiswa: three chibi engineering students in navy blue lab jackets, one boy with messy hair, one girl in a
pastel hijab, one boy with a cap, sitting at small desks with notebooks.
Elektron: a tiny round glowing yellow mascot with a happy face, representing electric current.
```

## Negatif (kalau tool-nya punya kolom negative prompt)

```
realistic, photorealistic, 3d render, creepy, extra fingers, deformed hands, blurry, messy text, watermark, dark gloomy
```

---

## Adegan

**1. Pembuka**
```
Pak Dosen waves happily in front of a big green chalkboard in a bright lecture hall; on the desk sits a cute toy
electric motor with a red magnet and a blue magnet; the three students raise their hands excitedly; sparkles and
small lightning-bolt doodles float around.
Dialog: "Selamat pagi semuanya! Hari ini kita lihat motor dan generator."
```

**2. Soal 2: gaya pada kumparan**
```
Close-up of a cute chibi motor: a copper rectangular coil between a red magnet labeled U and a blue magnet labeled
S, soft blue field lines flowing from the red magnet to the blue magnet; Elektron mascots run along the copper wire
in a loop; on one side of the coil a big green arrow pushes UP, on the other side a green arrow pushes DOWN, making
the coil spin; Pak Dosen points at the arrows with his stick; students go "ooh".
Dialog: "Satu sisi didorong ke atas, satu sisi ke bawah. Pasangan gaya inilah yang memutar kumparan."
```

**3. Komutator**
```
Zoomed-in cartoon view of a split copper ring (commutator) on the motor shaft with two black brushes touching it;
one half of the ring glows red, the other glows blue, and they swap as the coil turns; Elektron mascots jump from one
half to the other like playing hopscotch; Pak Dosen holds up a magnifying glass, a student takes notes.
Dialog: "Setiap setengah putaran, arusnya dibalik, jadi dorongannya selalu searah."
```

**4. Tanpa komutator**
```
The chibi coil wobbles back and forth like a swing, then stops standing upright, looking dizzy with swirly eyes;
the green force arrows point straight up and down in line with the coil; a student scratches her head with a
question mark above her; Pak Dosen laughs gently and explains.
Dialog: "Tanpa komutator, kumparan berhenti tegak. Torsinya nol."
```

**5. Poros ditahan (GGL balik)**
```
Pak Dosen grabs the motor shaft with both hands like holding a fishing rod; the copper coil turns red-hot with
cartoon steam puffs and sweat drops; Elektron mascots crowd and rush faster, looking panicked; a cute thermometer
pops up and shoots to the top; the students cover their mouths in surprise.
Dialog: "Porosnya ditahan, arus naik jadi tiga ampere. Itulah kenapa dinamo yang macet cepat panas!"
```

**6. Gaya Lorentz (F = BIL sin θ)**
```
A single copper wire floats between a red magnet and a blue magnet with blue field lines; a cute protractor
character shows the angle between the wire and the field; when the wire is perpendicular a big green arrow
points up, when the wire turns to 30 degrees the arrow shrinks to half, when the wire lies parallel to the field
lines the arrow disappears with a little "poof"; Pak Dosen holds a sign with a sine wave doodle.
Dialog: "Kalau kawat sejajar medan, gayanya nol. Jangan lupa sin teta, ya!"
```

**7. Soal 4: generator AC**
```
A student turns a big hand crank connected to a copper coil spinning between a red magnet and a blue magnet;
the blue field lines passing through the coil glow yellow; a light bulb with a happy face lights up brighter and
dimmer in rhythm; above them a cute wavy sine-wave ribbon flows like a rainbow; small icons of coal, a waterfall,
a geothermal steam vent, a wind turbine and a diesel jerry can orbit around.
Dialog: "Apa pun bahan bakarnya, tugasnya sama: memutar kumparan di dalam medan magnet."
```

**8. Fluks dan GGL**
```
Split comic panel: left panel, the coil stands upright with many yellow field lines passing through it, a happy
sleepy light bulb is OFF, label bubble "Φ maks, ε = 0"; right panel, the coil lies flat slicing quickly through
the field lines like a fan, the light bulb shines with a big smile, label bubble "Φ = 0, ε maks"; Pak Dosen stands
between the panels pointing at both.
```

**9. Putaran 2×**
```
The student turns the crank twice as fast with speed lines and a determined face; the sine-wave ribbon above
becomes twice as tall; the light bulb shines super bright with star eyes; Pak Dosen gives a thumbs up.
Dialog: "Putar dua kali lebih cepat, gelombangnya dua kali lebih tinggi."
```

**10. Frekuensi dan jumlah kutub**
```
Side-by-side chibi generators: a small fast-spinning rotor with 4 colorful poles (red and blue alternating) next to
a cute diesel generator set, and a big slow-spinning rotor with 24 poles next to a cartoon hydroelectric dam with a
waterfall; both send out identical wavy lines into a shared cute power plug labeled "50 Hz"; Pak Dosen holds a
calculator.
Dialog: "Empat kutub cepat atau dua puluh empat kutub pelan, hasilnya sama: lima puluh hertz."
```

**11. Genset 20 kVA (kVA bukan kW)**
```
A cute chubby generator-set character with a face, sweating as it carries a stack of appliance characters on its
head: a lamp and a fridge at the bottom, a water pump in the middle, an air conditioner on top; a dashed red
"safe limit" line glows just below the top; the student switches off the water pump, which hops away happily,
and the generator sighs with relief; Pak Dosen ticks a checkbox on the chalkboard.
Dialog: "Tiga belas kilowatt sudah lewat batas aman. Matikan pompanya, nah, aman!"
```

**12. Penutup**
```
Pak Dosen and the three students pose together waving goodbye; a cute motor and a cute generator character hold
hands like twins with a heart between them; confetti and small lightning bolts; warm sunset light through the
classroom window.
Dialog: "Ingat ya, motor dan generator adalah mesin yang sama. Sampai jumpa minggu depan!"
```

## Prompt gerak (untuk tool video seperti Kling/Runway/Pika/Veo)

Tambahkan di akhir prompt adegan:

```
gentle camera push-in, smooth looping animation, characters blink and bounce slightly, 5 seconds, 24 fps
```
